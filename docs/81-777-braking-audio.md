# 81-777 braking audio

Braking squeals are disabled: the notch-dependent brake squeals, near-stop
squeal clips, and the separate wheel/rail squeal layer no longer play.

The pneumatic brake-release clip `013_rd_release_low.wav` is retained. It
plays when the brake command is released after the train has come to a stop
(below 0.2 km/h). Compressor, door, motor, rolling, and rail-joint audio are
not changed.
